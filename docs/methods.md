# Methods / version 0.1

All analysis functions are pure numerical functions of supplied frames. Acquisition timestamps affect window selection, but analysis does not call clocks or generate randomness. Seeded simulator reproducibility additionally requires the same start timestamp. Analysis results include algorithm identifiers; rerun on the same data and dependency versions for identical numerical results.

- **S(L,T):** mean canonical sensor channels over the window. Magnetic flux density is T; acceleration m/s²; angular velocity rad/s; temperature K; humidity a 0–1 fraction; pressure Pa. Audio is normalized digital amplitude, not calibrated pressure.
- **C=(F,A,P):** remove the mean, apply a Hann window, use real FFT. Select the largest non-DC bin, use `2|FFT|/sum(window)` amplitude, and the bin's complex phase. A separate Welch PSD uses up to 256 samples per segment. Frequency resolution is constrained by the recording duration; V0.1 does not interpolate peaks.
- **H:** ratio of dominant frequencies. Near-integer harmonics use an absolute ratio tolerance of 0.05. This is a descriptor, not proof of a common physical source.
- **PR/COH/CPL:** predefined M_X/M_Y, M_X/A_X, A_X/A_Y, A_X/G_X pairs. Phase is the negative phase of the cross spectral density at a common bin closest to the first channel's dominant frequency. Magnitude-squared coherence uses overlapping segments of at most 64 samples, never a single whole-record segment. Coupling is Pearson correlation; cross-correlation lag is the peak of the demeaned full correlation. Undefined coherence/coupling on constant signals is null.
- **FM:** S, C, R plus a feature vector: 12 means, 12 standard deviations, 9 motion/magnetic dominant frequencies, audio RMS, audio dominant frequency. At least 80 frames are required; inter-frame timing deviating by more than 10% from the declared rate yields an explicit resampling-required state, rather than fabricated results. V0.1 does not resample irregular hardware data.
- **Normalization:** fixed scales in channel order: 2 µT, 0.03 m/s², 0.004 rad/s for their three-axis groups; 1 K, 0.01 humidity fraction, 100 Pa; 1 Hz per motion frequency; 0.01 audio RMS and 1000 Hz acoustic frequency. These engineering scales must be revisited for calibrated hardware. Similarity is `exp(-RMS(normalized_difference))`; anomaly score is that RMS distance. Neither is a calibrated probability.
- **PI:** median feature vector across at least three completed, unperturbed baseline runs, each with at least eight seconds of qualifying data, in the same room and source mode. Repeatability score `1/(1+mean(normalized_std))` is stored as heuristic confidence. Conditions and run count are retained. This modular V0.1 estimator is not evidence of a persistent physical location identity. Device/calibration equivalence is not yet enforced across separate physical baseline sessions: do not combine different devices/calibrations.
- **D:** dimensionless, fixed-scale normalized FM feature differences from the PI. The additive FM=PI+D interpretation applies in normalized feature space, not to the entire nested map.
- **PA:** versioned encoded PI, D, relationships and normalized FM vector with a deterministic SHA-256 identifier. Without PI, it is explicitly preliminary. It is not a geographic address, authentication token, or validated unique signature.
- **RFN:** timestamp-selected pre/during/post maps; stored physical-unit feature differences and normalized comparisons (`post-pre`, `during-pre`), with residual vs pre and change since during as recovery descriptors. This is an endpoint recovery comparison, not a fitted recovery-time constant. Short/invalid windows produce insufficient status. Input frequency, end frequency, digital amplitude, duration, software onset/offset, and actuation source are stored.
- **Room comparison:** latest qualifying completed FM in each room for the selected source; normalized feature difference, similarity, largest differing features, common/differing pair coherence and wrapped phase deltas. Shared relationship thresholds are coherence delta <0.1 and phase delta <0.2 rad. PI, PA, and stored RFNs are included. Different environmental conditions remain possible confounders; collecting many independent runs is necessary before interpreting repeatability.

No measurements are generated to fill missing hardware channels. Hardware calibration, anti-aliasing, timebase characterization, uncertainty propagation, sensor noise floors, statistically validated PI confidence, and physical actuator latency require bench work before scientific conclusions.
